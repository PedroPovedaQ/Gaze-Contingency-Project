# Eye dot — 2026-10-25

## Implemented

- Headset Settings has an **Eye dot: On/Off** button, available in rotational layouts and off on component creation. Toggle stays independent of the eye-ray line and wall guides.
- `EyeDot` consumes `SharedGazeStream.TryGetValid`, including its alignment and freshness policy. It draws only during active search; no marker during pause, readiness, transitions or invalid gaze.
- `FindObjectGameManager.TryEyeDotPoint` intersects all eight bounded object-center planes and chooses the nearest positive hit. It uses current seated translation/yaw, radius, floor and wall height. Empty spaces between objects still show the dot.
- Cyan 18 mm disc, stereo-aware URP shader retained via Resources, depth overlay so object meshes cannot obscure the projected point. No collider or selection component; Ignore Raycast layer. Mesh/material allocated once and released on destruction.
- Raw gaze observations record `eyeDotEnabled`. This is visual feedback, not calibration or a research accuracy metric. Keep setting consistent in comparative study runs.
