\action{RESEARCHER ONLY}{Two counterbalanced voice blocks: one uses the participant-preferred voice and the other uses the self-similar voice. Each participant experiences both voices; use the saved assigned order. A block comprises multiple trials, and each trial is one target-object search. Both will use gaze-contingent directional guidance. Before each search, the participant will charge a world-fixed readiness cross on the previous target wall; the first practice will use the front wall. Say ``first block'' and ``second block''; do not suggest that either voice should improve performance.}

\step{1 Welcome and consent}
\say{SAY}{``Thank you for coming. Today you will be performing a search task in mixed reality. In this task, you will stay seated and turn to find virtual objects by their color and shape. A voice assistant will provide hints. You will be performing this task for a number of trials over two task blocks, each using a different assistant voice. You may pause, take a break or stop at any time without penalty.''}
\say{SAY}{``We will ask you to record a short voice sample to create a synthetic version of your voice. We will also record eye-gaze and task data under a study code. Please read the consent form for what will be collected, where it will be processed and how it will be handled.''}

\action{ACTION}{Obtain consent.}
\step{2 Initial Qualtrics and Voice Setup}

\say{SAY}{``Please complete the background and current-comfort questions before we begin. Use only the study code already entered. Do not add your name or contact details. You may skip optional questions. We will also ask what kind of assistant voice you would prefer. There are no right or wrong preferences.''}
\action{ACTION}{Confirm the coded participant and run IDs. Include the baseline demographics and current-comfort items, followed by the separate questions below. Keep skipped responses distinct from ``no preference.''}

\say{SURVEY ITEM / AGENT GENDER PREFERENCE}{``What gender presentation would you prefer for the agent's voice?''}
\say{SURVEY ITEM / AGENT ACCENT PREFERENCE}{``What accent would you prefer the agent's voice to have?''}

\action{ACTION / CANDIDATE PREPARATION}{Save the requested preferences and configure the selected voice for the participant-preferred block.}
\say{SAY}{``We will now record the short voice sample described in the consent form. After the tone, please read the displayed passage in your usual speaking voice.''}

\say{ASK / AUDIO CHECK}{``Can you hear the voice clearly and at a comfortable volume?''}

\step{3 Fit and calibrate the headset}
\say{SAY}{``Remain seated in this chair. You may turn the chair, your body and your head to look around, but do not stand or walk. Tell me immediately if you feel dizzy, nauseated, strained, uncomfortable or unsteady.''}
\action{ACTION}{Fit equipment and verify tracking, perform the automated eye-calibration protocol.}

\say{ASK}{``Can you see clearly? Does the equipment feel comfortable? Do you feel well enough to continue?''}
\step{4. Pre-Block Activity}
\say{SAY}{``First, I will explain the task. In each trial, you will search for one object. The agent will tell you which object to find, for example, `Locate the blue sphere.' You will point the controller ray at the matching object to highlight it, then press the trigger to select it. Selecting the correct object will complete the trial; if you select an incorrect object, you will hear an error ding- continue searching for the same target. You will then continue over several more rounds with the agent asking you locate one object in each round.''}
\say{SAY}{``If you would like a rest, let me know. We will normally take it between trials, before the next search begins. You can also ask us to pause or stop immediately at any time.''}
\action{BREAKS AND TIMING}{Requested rest breaks normally occur after a trial ends and before the next search begins. Do not start the next search until the participant is ready. Breaks and questionnaires are outside active-search time. If a participant needs to pause during a trial, pause immediately, suspend active-search timing and record the interruption and its duration separately. Retain the interrupted-trial record; do not silently replace it or count break time as search time.}

\step{6 First task block}
\action{CURRENT SCHEDULE / PROTOCOL PROPOSAL}{10 experimental trials in this voice block; practice trials are separate. Each absolute target-wall angle ($|\theta| = 0^\circ, 45^\circ, 90^\circ, 135^\circ, 180^\circ$) will occur twice. Angles will be measured from the readiness cross on the previous target wall to the next target wall center. Verify the installed build schedule before participant use.}
\action{IMPLEMENTED READINESS GATE / PILOT VERIFICATION}{Before each trial, the participant will look at the entire readiness cross until its left-to-right fill completes. The cross will stay at the center of the previous target wall. One second of continuous valid gaze within its padded area and completion of the target announcement will both be required. Looking away, tracking loss or pausing will reset the fill. Search timing will begin only when the objects appear. Confirm headset usability before participant use.}
\action{ANGLE ASSIGNMENT AND RECORDING}{Trial order and object heights will be randomized using recorded seeds. The second block will mirror left/right wall assignments and independently shuffle trial order while preserving the same absolute-angle counts. For example, a start wall at $315^\circ$ and target wall at $0^\circ$ will give signed $\theta=+45^\circ$ and $|\theta|=45^\circ$. Confirm that the trial schedule and actual object positions are saved; exact object bearings and measured head rotation are separate from wall-angle categories.}
\action{RESEARCHER SETTINGS}{Confirm the saved first voice and planned trial schedule. Keep object layout, selection method, guidance policy and the finalized prompt wording constant across voices.}
\say{SAY}{``We will now begin the first task block. Find each target as quickly and accurately as you comfortably can. Stay seated and use the same controller ray and trigger selection you practiced. Tell me if you need to pause.''}
\action{ACTION}{Start block; monitor safety.}
\say{AT COMPLETION SAY}{``That task block is complete. Please stop and lower your hands comfortably.''}
\say{SAY}{``The next questions will ask about your workload, your experience of the activity and the agent. Please answer based on the block you just finished.''}
\action{ACTION}{Administer Survey Block \#1.}
\say{SAY}{``You may take a short break of up to 5 minutes. Tell me when you are ready to continue.''}
\say{BEFORE CONTINUING ASK}{``Do you feel comfortable and well enough to continue?''}
\step{7 Second task block}
\action{CURRENT SCHEDULE / PROTOCOL PROPOSAL}{10 experimental trials using the other assigned voice, with two trials at each absolute target-wall angle, for 20 experimental trials across both blocks. Retain incomplete or interrupted trials with their outcomes; do not add replacement trials merely to reach 10 valid attempts.}
\say{SAY}{``We will now begin the second task block. This block will use the other assistant voice. The task and selection method will remain the same. Find each target as quickly and accurately as you comfortably can, and tell me if you need to pause.''}
\action{ACTION}{Start block; monitor safety.}
\say{AT COMPLETION SAY}{``The search task is complete. Please stop turning. We will finish the questions about this block, then I will help you remove the headset.''}
\say{SAY}{``The next questions will ask about your workload, your experience of the activity and the agent. Please answer based on the block you just finished.''}
\action{ACTION}{Administer Survey Block \#2.}
\clearpage
\step{8 Final Qualtrics}
\say{SAY}{``Please complete the final questionnaire. It will ask about your experiences with the two voices.''}
\say{SAY}{``Some follow-up questions may appear depending on your answers about the two voices. Answer based on your own experience and tell me when you have submitted it.''}
\action{EXPLORATORY FOLLOW-UP}{After open responses, ask ``Did either voice feel like your own thoughts, like another person helping, or neither?'' Record this as an exploratory report, not proof of an internal-dialogue mechanism.}
\action{ACTION}{Administer Post-Study Survey Block}
\step{9 Debrief and finish}
\say{SAY}{``Thank you. This study compares participant-preferred and self-similar voices during gaze-guided object search. Both voices provide gaze-contingent assistance. We are examining task performance and how participants experience the activity and assistant.''}
\say{SAY}{``Your data are stored under a coded participant ID. Please avoid discussing the voice conditions or study details with people who may participate later, because knowing them in advance could affect their behavior.''}
\say{ASK}{``Do you have any questions about the study or your experience?''}
\say{SAY}{``Thank you for participating. We are finished.''}
