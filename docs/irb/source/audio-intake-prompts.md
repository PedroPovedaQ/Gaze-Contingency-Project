# Audio Intake Prompts

Study: **Follow My Voice: Assisted Gaze-Contingent Search with a Self-Similar Agent in Virtual Reality**

These prompts are read aloud by a synthetic intake voice after consent. A tone follows each prompt, and the participant answers aloud. Answers are audio recorded.

## Eligibility

1. Are you 18 years of age or older? After the tone, please answer in a full sentence.
2. Can you hear and understand this voice at a comfortable volume? After the tone, please answer in a full sentence.
3. With your usual glasses or contact lenses if needed, can you see clearly enough to read and to find small objects? After the tone, please answer in a full sentence.
4. For this experiment, you will be required to stay seated, turn in a swivel chair, and use a controller in your right hand. Can you safely do this? After the tone, please answer in a full sentence. You do not need to give medical details.

## Background and Experience

5. Tell me about any experience you have had with virtual reality. What have you tried, how often, and how did it feel? If you have never tried it, tell me what you imagine it is like.
6. Tell me how you use voice assistants or spoken directions in everyday life, such as on your phone or while driving. What works well for you, and what is frustrating?
7. Have you used eye tracking before? If you have, describe it. If not, how would you feel about a system that knows where you are looking?
8. Are you wearing glasses, contact lenses, or no visual correction today? No prescription details are needed.

## Speech Sample

9. Imagine you are helping a friend find something they lost in a room. Talk them through it for about thirty seconds, telling them where to look, which way to turn, and when they are getting close.

## Optional Reading Passage

Used only if the answers above do not provide enough speech.

Locate the red sphere. Look toward the upper shelf. Move your gaze slowly to the right. Check the blue cube near the center. Compare each object's color and shape. Scan the lower row from left to right. Look beside the green cylinder. Point the controller ray at the target and check its highlight. Press the trigger to select the matching object. Take a short pause. Get ready for the next round.
