## IMI specification and source transfer

**Protocol proposal — confirmed:** include the Intrinsic Motivation Inventory. **Protocol proposal — working selection:** use the four dimensions used by Kao et al. (2021), §4.3.3, after each voice block: interest/enjoyment, effort/importance, pressure/tension, value/usefulness. This is not the packet's separate 22-item version, which contains a different set of subscales.

The [official complete packet](../resources/surveys/imi-complete.pdf) contains 7, 5, 5 and 7 items respectively in these full item-bank subscales (24 responses per block; 48 over two blocks). This is our proposed full-bank selection, not a claim that Kao et al. administered exactly 24 items. Keep “activity” referring to the just-completed search block. Final wording, the value/usefulness fill-ins, item order and survey burden remain **Open decisions**; archive a versioned item map before participant use.

Use the official seven-point truth anchors. Reverse-keyed items use `8 - response`; average within each subscale. In the full-bank ordering, reverse interest/enjoyment items 3 and 4, effort/importance items 2 and 5, and pressure/tension items 1 and 3; value/usefulness has no reverse-keyed items. Verify keys against the actual frozen form after any item selection/reordering. Do not compute one total IMI score. Only interest/enjoyment directly represents intrinsic motivation; other subscales represent distinct experiences. Source: [official IMI overview](https://selfdeterminationtheory.org/intrinsic-motivation-inventory/) and saved packet.

**Protocol proposal — missingness rule:** retain raw responses and missing counts; score a subscale only if all selected items are answered, otherwise mark that score missing. Do not invent a response or substitute another participant's mean. Review this conservative project rule before analysis freeze; it is not presented as an official IMI requirement.

**Reported evidence:** Kao et al. used IMI in an educational programming game; §5.2.5 reports no significant direct voice-similarity effect on the four IMI outcomes. Their PENS competence/relatedness/immersion outcomes are separate measures, not IMI subscales adopted here. Their voluntary time played is not equivalent to time needed to find an object. [Paper and provenance](../resources/surveys/README.md).

## Collection, joining and analysis contract

**Protocol proposal:** before task, collect background and baseline symptoms. After each block, remove headset for the planned break and collect TLX, IMI, voice ratings/manipulation check and technical checks about that block, before discussing the next voice. End with post-exposure symptoms, preference and interview. Freeze order and pilot total burden; revalidate tracking after re-donning.

Survey exports need `participant_id`, `session_id`/`run_number`, `block`, `voice_condition`, `condition_order`, `instrument_version`, `item_id`, raw response, missing reason where volunteered, and start/end timestamps. Operator-only condition labels should not prime answers. Behavioral exports additionally need `trial_id`, practice flag, geometry, outcome and quality flags. Preserve raw values, units, clock domains and a reproducible scoring map; wall-clock survey times do not directly join Unity elapsed times without a mapping.

Compare voice conditions within participants; retain order and geometry/repetition information. Keep practice separate. Do not pool legacy awareness conditions with the voice-only study. No automated exclusion for a failed subjective manipulation check. Preregister primary outcome, missingness/exclusion rules, multiplicity treatment and aggregation/model before confirmatory analysis. Current [analysis workflow](../analysis/README.md) does not yet implement this entire register.
