# Consent-first audio intake and study setup

**Implemented — October 28, 2026.** Protocol identifier: `audio-intake-v2-20261028`. This replaces the October 25 interview prototype. Software verification does not establish participant, microphone, voice-similarity or wearer validation.

## Operator sequence

1. Launch `~/Applications/Follow My Voice Study Intake.app` (the executable also accepts `--intake` to open intake directly). Connect the Focus Vision and start Trial Viewer Live reception. Keep the Mac awake and connected throughout Local MiniLM use. Start with the headset waiting at setup.
2. Open **New participant session**. Reserve an unused P###, enter optional manually measured participant IPD and headset lens spacing, and use the assigned counterbalance: **A = Self-Similar Voice first; B = Selected Voice first**. The operator enters the preassigned order; the intake does not randomize it. A duplicate desktop participant code is rejected, and the headset separately rejects conflicting existing participant ownership/order. Resume a saved intake instead of overwriting it.
3. Complete the approved signed-consent process outside the microphone. Enter researcher initials and the signed document/version or record reference, then attest separately to study consent and authorization for recording, ElevenLabs transcription/cloning and synthesis. **Record consent receipt** persists the participant/session-bound UTC receipt. This attestation is not an electronic participant signature and does not establish institutional approval. No microphone recording, transcription or cloning is enabled before it exists.
4. Let the participant audition catalog choices and confirm their **Selected Voice**. The twelve existing voices remain available. The fixed intake agent is **River**, ElevenLabs voice ID `SAz9YHcvj6GT2YYXdXww`, outside that catalog. River cannot be assigned to either experimental condition. This separation avoids reusing a condition voice during intake; it does not prove absence of priming.
5. River speaks each fixed question. After playback completes, select **Record participant answer**. A 180 ms, 880 Hz tone cues speech. Capture begins after the tone (250 ms from tone start), excluding cue and prompt from the answer sample. Press **Stop and submit answer**. Use headphones and a quiet room. Microphone capture is researcher-controlled, limited to 120 seconds per answer, and never runs silently in the background.
6. Finish all nine fixed eligibility and background questions first. Baseline items can be skipped and remain marked missing. If the totals are below 30 seconds of estimated speech or 50 transcript words, Audio intake offers the optional reading fallback or **Continue to eligibility review**. Any fallback takes are collected before review. The researcher then listens/reads and records Yes / No / Unclear for each of the four eligibility items with reviewer initials, once. All four must be Yes and none skipped. Otherwise screening stops; all already-recorded answers are retained under the existing approved handling procedure, with no clone or cache.
7. Immediately after a positive review, the clone worker starts automatically if the samples contain at least 30 seconds of estimated speech and 50 transcript words. Usable speech from all nine answers and any fallback takes counts; no cloning starts during intake or before review. If the operator continued to review below either threshold, cloning remains blocked. The exact submitted-answer snapshot is frozen and hashed. These are conservative engineering admission gates, not provider-certified adequacy or validated voice quality measures.
8. Once the clone is ready, Trial Viewer automatically prepares both full runtime phrase libraries in its participant workspace. Both voice IDs, exact request texts, runtime template hash, MP3 hashes and decoded-cache hashes are recorded. The headset exports the authoritative template; desktop code does not guess trial wording. Wait for every clip, then **Transfer and verify study audio on headset**. Interrupted preparation can resume; existing headset MP3s are never overwritten. A hash conflict blocks handoff and preserves both copies.
9. **Send session to headset for confirmation** becomes available only after completed eligible intake, cloning and verified transfer. The wearer confirms P### and condition order. The headset verifies the participant-bound manifest, current phrase-template identity and cache presence, then applies the usual decoding, clone treatment, level matching and two-voice listening acceptance. The desktop caches raw provider audio and decoded PCM, not a claim of accepted acoustic quality. The ordinary headset **Start setup** now directs study participants through this consent-first route; explicitly labelled development quick tests remain separate.
10. Run standard gaze preparation: Start once → three fit dots → three-second transition → five verification dots → result / Retry / explicit Skip without offset → three-object look-and-right-trigger activation → Center and begin. See [the accuracy method](../research/standard-gaze-accuracy-method.md). No new eye threshold or trial-selection method is introduced by intake.
11. Run one uncounted practice using the first assigned voice, then two counterbalanced 32-trial blocks with the existing between-block rest/comfort/tracking checkpoint. The 25-second active-search timeout remains. Only right-ray/trigger selection captures an object.
12. After trial 80, remove the headset with researcher assistance, check symptoms/comfort, and administer the external final comparison survey labelled **Selected Voice | Self-Similar Voice**. A unique `final-survey-handoff-<id>.json` in the run folder records P###, desktop session, run, block-to-label mapping and selected voice name. It says `awaiting_external_survey`, `responsesCollected=false`; no survey completion is invented. The corresponding event is `final_survey_handoff`, not an in-headset NASA-TLX prompt. The handoff includes the URL-encoded fields supplied by the survey workflow for `SV_3retnpdBMwBx5bw`, version `v2026-10-28c DRAFT`: `participant_id`, `session_id`, `run_id`, `condition_order`, `selected_voice_block`, `self_similar_voice_block`, `selected_voice_name`, `handoff_id`. In Trial Viewer, use **Find this participant's completed-run survey handoff** to display/copy the matching link after Wi-Fi reception. The survey is unpublished; the study team must publish it before participant use. No response is submitted automatically.

## Exact fixed question list

1. **adult:** “Are you 18 years of age or older? Please answer yes or no after the tone.”
2. **hearing:** “Can you hear and understand this spoken guidance at a comfortable volume? Please answer yes or no after the tone.”
3. **vision:** “With your usual glasses or contact lenses if needed, can you see the scene clearly? Please answer yes, no, or not sure after the tone.”
4. **seated_controller:** “Can you safely remain seated, turn in the chair, and point and press the trigger with the right controller? Please answer yes, no, or not sure after the tone. You do not need to give medical details.”
5. **vr:** “How often have you used virtual reality: never, less than monthly, monthly, weekly, or daily? After the tone, describe what you have tried and how comfortable you felt using it.”
6. **voice_assistant:** “How often do you use voice assistants: never, less than monthly, monthly, weekly, or daily? After the tone, describe what you use them for and your experience following spoken instructions.”
7. **eye_tracking:** “Have you used eye tracking before? After the tone, describe your experience, or say that you have not used it.”
8. **handedness:** “Which hand do you usually prefer for everyday tasks: right, left, or both equally? Answer after the tone.”
9. **visual_correction:** “Are you using no visual correction, glasses, contact lenses, or another correction for this session? Answer after the tone. No prescription details are needed.”

No name, diagnosis or prescription is requested. No generative follow-up is used. Raw transcript text remains ASR output, not automatically certified fact. Eligibility values are separately researcher-reviewed; baseline answers remain topic-keyed transcripts, with missing/skipped states rather than invented numeric codes.

## Reading fallback decision

**Implemented:** retain the old task-command passage as an explicit fallback after all nine questions and before researcher eligibility review when speech is insufficient. After a short or skipped fallback, the operator can add another take or continue to review; review is recorded only once. The stepper remains on Audio intake until this choice is complete, then shows **Audio intake ✓ → Eligibility review → Clone & cache**. It is labelled `reading_fallback`, uses the same consent and tone gates, and is saved as an additional take. It is not a new mandatory reading task. It preserves historical wording (including shelf/color examples); those words are not descriptions of the current hidden-target task:

> Locate the red sphere. Look toward the upper shelf. Move your gaze slowly to the right. Check the blue cube near the center. Compare each object's color and shape. Scan the lower row from left to right. Look beside the green cylinder. Point the controller ray at the target and check its highlight. Press the trigger to select the matching object. Take a short pause. Get ready for the next round.

Speech estimate uses 20 ms mono PCM windows, removes each window's DC mean and counts RMS ≥0.01 full scale. Empty transcripts and ≥1% clipped samples are excluded from cloning. This energy heuristic cannot distinguish speech from all background sounds. Researcher listening and subsequent headset audio acceptance remain necessary. The worker stores the quality method and values; it never labels an energy estimate as validated voice quality.

## Storage and recovery

**Implemented:** private desktop workspace `~/Documents/Trial Review Intake/<session UUID>/`, owner-only directory, contains `session.json`, immutable consent/eligibility receipts, question-specific capture authorization, original answer WAVs, optional multichannel `.source.wav` plus mono downmix, transcripts/quality/hash receipts, prompt/audition MP3s, clone input hash and answer IDs, `audio-cache/`, uniquely named audio manifests and a minimal assignment. The participant code is in state/manifests; the UUID prevents accidental directory reuse. Do not delete source recordings or provider voices as part of retries.

The clone uses a frozen snapshot of usable participant answer WAVs only. Prompts, tones, auditions and researcher comments are excluded. ASR is ElevenLabs `scribe_v2`; prompts and study synthesis use `eleven_turbo_v2_5`; cloning uses its existing instant voice-cloning endpoint. No answer goes to Mistral in this protocol. Credentials remain in the existing private `~/.config/follow-my-voice/api_keys.json`, outside source/app bundles.

The headset receives the minimal schema-2 assignment, full generated study-audio library and manifest. It does not receive raw answers, transcripts, consent-document reference, researcher initials or eligibility explanations. Consent ID, protocol version, reviewed-eligibility flag, cache manifest hash and development flag provide linkage. Participant/run `intake-assignment-v2.json` and summary `intake_assignment` preserve this provenance.

Closing/reopening the panel preserves worker results and saved state. Failed transcription can retry the saved answer without double-counting it. Uncertain clone outcomes do not auto-retry; the explicit retry warns of a possible duplicate provider voice. Worker failure does not authorize handoff. Withdrawal disables subsequent capture, synthesis and handoff but cannot undo a request already sent to a provider; retained data follows the approved handling process. Existing older intake sessions are read-only in this new flow because they lack a prospective consent receipt.

Development rehearsals must select **Development rehearsal** when reserving the session. Headset summaries use `session_mode=development_intake`, `study_data=false`, and retain the full two-block study path for testing. These are not participant observations. Ordinary quick-test modes remain separate.

**Implemented:** schema-2 study intakes lock guidance to Local MiniLM in both voice blocks; the Jev switch remains a development comparison control. Bundle `20261026-minilm-reassurance-v8` uses policy `jev_adaptive_assistance_v4`. The v8 data manifest records authored synthetic action-policy examples (2,240 train / 448 validation / 448 test); recorded device probes are evaluation evidence, not training rows. Existing deterministic safety and qualified-event paths remain. No model was retrained for this intake change.
