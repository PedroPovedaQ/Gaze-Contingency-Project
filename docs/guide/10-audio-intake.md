# Consent-first audio intake and study setup

**Implemented — November 1, 2026.** Protocol identifier: `audio-intake-v3-20261101`. New sessions use ten fixed questions. Historical v2 sessions keep their original nine-question protocol and remain readable without migration. Software verification does not establish participant, microphone, voice-similarity or wearer validation.

**Protocol decision — November 1, 2026:** initial enrollment target is 20 participants, 10 per assigned order. Maintain the counterbalance roster outside the app; intake accepts the researcher's assignment and does not enforce an enrollment cap. A = Self-Similar first; B = Selected first. Retain the clone-only inner-thought treatment and the existing left/right guidance for rearward targets; no spoken “behind you” cue is added. See [participant readiness and rehearsal checks](../voice-study-qa.md).

## Operator sequence

1. Launch `~/Applications/Follow My Voice Study Intake.app` (the executable also accepts `--intake` to open intake directly). Connect the Focus Vision and start Trial Viewer Live reception. Keep the Mac awake and connected throughout Local MiniLM use. Start with the headset waiting at setup.
2. Open **New participant session**. Reserve the suggested next unused P### and use the assigned counterbalance: **A = Self-Similar Voice first; B = Selected Voice first**. The code stays editable; duplicates are warned and rejected. Numbering checks intake folders, its persistent reservation ledger, catalog identity/history, local headset archives and the headset's next-unused suggestion. Stopped/withdrawn codes remain reserved; **Finish here / create another session** refreshes without restarting. New development rehearsals use the separate **P900000000001** sequence and remain excluded from study data. The operator enters the preassigned order; intake does not randomize it. Resume a saved intake instead of overwriting it. Reserve has no IPD fields: passive headset setup supplies separately labelled view separation and an unvalidated eye-origin separation estimate. See [automatic fit](../research/automatic-headset-fit.md) for sources, provenance, warnings and manual fallback.
3. Complete the approved signed-consent process outside the microphone. Enter researcher initials and the signed document/version or record reference, then attest separately to study consent and authorization for recording, ElevenLabs transcription/cloning and synthesis. **Record consent receipt** persists the participant/session-bound UTC receipt. This attestation is not an electronic participant signature and does not establish institutional approval. No microphone recording, transcription or cloning is enabled before it exists.
4. Let the participant audition catalog choices and confirm their **Selected Voice**. The twelve existing voices remain available. The fixed intake agent is **River**, ElevenLabs voice ID `SAz9YHcvj6GT2YYXdXww`, outside that catalog. River cannot be assigned to either experimental condition. This separation avoids reusing a condition voice during intake; it does not prove absence of priming.
5. Confirming **Selected Voice / begin intake** starts the automatic interview once. River speaks each question, then a 180 ms, 880 Hz tone plays; microphone capture begins 250 ms after tone onset. Each answer is saved and transcribed before the next question. Exact-text local River MP3s are reused when available; missing prompts use ElevenLabs. Prompt/provider/playback failure pauses with a visible error; it never silently skips a question. Use headphones and a quiet room. **Pause intake**, **Repeat question**, **Skip this question**, and **Stop intake / record withdrawal** remain visible. Pause ends capture immediately and preserves a partial take locally: **Save paused answer / continue** submits it; Repeat starts a fresh take. Resume/reopen never silently restarts the microphone. A control selected while an answer is being saved waits for that result and never skips the following question. Withdrawal is persisted independently of a running transcription; a request already sent cannot be recalled.

   **Implemented local activity settings:** 20 ms PCM windows at 24 kHz, mono downmix and DC removal, RMS ≥0.01 full scale, 200 ms consecutive activity for speech onset. Once speech is detected, stop after **3 seconds** of silence for questions 1–4 or **5 seconds** for questions 5–10 and fallback. Speech resets the silence clock. With no detected speech in **10 seconds**, replay the same question once, with a new tone/capture; a second no-speech timeout marks the answer unanswered and continues. All attempts remain local with capture receipts. Maximum **120 seconds** per take. Settings are in `session.json` and each capture authorization; automatic end receipts record reason and silence window. These are engineering settings tested with synthetic signals, not validated speech segmentation. Background noise can hold capture open; quiet speech can be missed. A full wearer rehearsal remains an **Open decision / validation requirement**; natural pauses longer than the chosen window can still terminate an answer.
6. Finish all ten fixed eligibility and background questions first. Baseline items can be skipped and remain marked missing. If the totals are below 30 seconds of estimated speech or 50 transcript words, Audio intake offers the optional reading fallback or **Continue to eligibility review**. Any fallback takes are collected before review. The researcher then listens/reads and records Yes / No / Unclear for each of the four eligibility items with reviewer initials, once. All four must be Yes and none skipped. Otherwise screening stops; all already-recorded answers are retained under the existing approved handling procedure, with no clone or cache.
7. Immediately after a positive review, the clone worker starts automatically if the samples contain at least 30 seconds of estimated speech and 50 transcript words. Usable speech from all ten answers and any fallback takes counts; no cloning starts during intake or before review. If the operator continued to review below either threshold, cloning remains blocked. The exact submitted-answer snapshot is frozen and hashed. These are conservative engineering admission gates, not provider-certified adequacy or validated voice quality measures.
8. Once the clone is ready, Trial Viewer automatically prepares both full runtime phrase libraries in its participant workspace. Both voice IDs, exact request texts, runtime template hash, MP3 hashes and decoded-cache hashes are recorded. The headset exports the authoritative template; desktop code does not guess trial wording. Wait for every clip, then **Transfer and verify study audio on headset**. Interrupted preparation can resume; existing headset MP3s are never overwritten. A hash conflict blocks handoff and preserves both copies.
9. **Send session to headset for confirmation** becomes available only after completed eligible intake, cloning and verified transfer. The wearer confirms P### and condition order. The headset verifies the participant-bound manifest, current phrase-template identity and cache presence, then applies the usual decoding, clone treatment, level matching and two-voice listening acceptance. The desktop caches raw provider audio and decoded PCM, not a claim of accepted acoustic quality. The ordinary headset **Start setup** now directs study participants through this consent-first route; explicitly labelled development quick tests remain separate.
10. Run standard gaze preparation: Start once → three fit dots → three-second transition → five verification dots → result / Retry / explicit Skip without offset → three-object look-and-right-trigger activation → Center and begin. See [the accuracy method](../research/standard-gaze-accuracy-method.md). No new eye threshold or trial-selection method is introduced by intake.
11. Run one uncounted practice using the first assigned voice, then two counterbalanced 48-trial blocks with the existing between-block rest/comfort/tracking checkpoint. The 25-second active-search timeout remains. Only right-ray/trigger selection captures an object.
12. After trial 96, remove the headset with researcher assistance, check symptoms/comfort, and administer the external final comparison survey labelled **Selected Voice | Self-Similar Voice**. A unique `final-survey-handoff-<id>.json` in the run folder records P###, desktop session, run, block-to-label mapping and selected voice name. It says `awaiting_external_survey`, `responsesCollected=false`; no survey completion is invented. The corresponding event is `final_survey_handoff`, not an in-headset NASA-TLX prompt. The handoff includes the URL-encoded fields supplied by the survey workflow for `SV_3retnpdBMwBx5bw`, version `v2026-10-28c DRAFT`: `participant_id`, `session_id`, `run_id`, `condition_order`, `selected_voice_block`, `self_similar_voice_block`, `selected_voice_name`, `handoff_id`. In Trial Viewer, use **Find this participant's completed-run survey handoff** to display/copy the matching link after Wi-Fi reception. The runtime still marks this survey unpublished/draft; the study team must confirm the released URL/version and update that contract before participant use. No response is submitted automatically.

## Exact fixed question list

1. **adult:** “Are you 18 years of age or older? After the tone, please answer in a full sentence.”
2. **hearing:** “Can you hear and understand this voice at a comfortable volume? After the tone, please answer in a full sentence.”
3. **vision:** “With your usual glasses or contact lenses if needed, can you see clearly enough to read and to find small objects? After the tone, please answer in a full sentence.”
4. **seated_controller:** “Can you safely stay seated, turn in a swivel chair, and use a controller in your right hand? After the tone, please answer in a full sentence. You do not need to give medical details.”
5. **vr:** “Tell me about any experience you have had with virtual reality. What have you tried, how often, and how did it feel? If you have never tried it, tell me what you imagine it is like.”
6. **voice_assistant:** “Tell me how you use voice assistants or spoken directions in everyday life, such as on your phone or while driving. What works well for you, and what is frustrating?”
7. **eye_tracking:** “Have you used eye tracking before? If you have, describe it. If not, how would you feel about a system that knows where you are looking?”
8. **handedness:** “Which hand do you prefer for everyday tasks, and what is something you always do with that hand?”
9. **visual_correction:** “Are you wearing glasses, contact lenses, or no visual correction today? Tell me how they usually feel when you are looking at screens. No prescription details are needed.”
10. **directional_speech:** “Imagine you are helping a friend find something they lost in a room. Talk them through it for about thirty seconds, telling them where to look, which way to turn, and when they are getting close.”

No name, diagnosis or prescription is requested. No generative follow-up is used. Raw transcript text remains ASR output, not automatically certified fact. Eligibility values are separately researcher-reviewed; all ten answers retain full transcripts. Questions 5–9 also retain researcher-reviewed categories: VR / voice-assistant frequency (never, less than monthly, monthly, weekly, daily, unclear), eye-tracking experience (yes/no/unclear), hand preference (right/left/both equally/unclear), and visual correction (none/glasses/contact lenses/other/unclear). These default to Unclear until researcher confirmation; frequency is never guessed from a narrative. No additional narrative interpretation or medical details are coded.

## Reading fallback decision

**Implemented:** retain the old task-command passage as an explicit fallback after all ten questions and before researcher eligibility review when speech is insufficient. After a short or skipped fallback, the operator can add another take or continue to review; review is recorded only once. The stepper remains on Audio intake until this choice is complete, then shows **Audio intake ✓ → Eligibility review → Clone & cache**. It is labelled `reading_fallback`, uses the same consent and tone gates, and is saved as an additional take. It is not a new mandatory reading task. It preserves historical wording (including shelf/color examples); those words are not descriptions of the current hidden-target task:

> Locate the red sphere. Look toward the upper shelf. Move your gaze slowly to the right. Check the blue cube near the center. Compare each object's color and shape. Scan the lower row from left to right. Look beside the green cylinder. Point the controller ray at the target and check its highlight. Press the trigger to select the matching object. Take a short pause. Get ready for the next round.

Speech estimate uses 20 ms mono PCM windows, removes each window's DC mean and counts RMS ≥0.01 full scale. Empty transcripts and ≥1% clipped samples are excluded from cloning. This energy heuristic cannot distinguish speech from all background sounds. Researcher listening and subsequent headset audio acceptance remain necessary. The worker stores the quality method and values; it never labels an energy estimate as validated voice quality.

## Transcripts and participant data dictionary

**Implemented:** every recorded answer, including eligibility, background, directional speech and fallback, is sent only to ElevenLabs `scribe_v2` for transcription. ASR failure is recorded without losing audio or blocking advancement. During eligibility review the researcher can read/listen to every answer, retry ASR, type a missing answer, correct a transcript and confirm a background category. Save corrections before the final eligibility review; that review freezes clone inputs. Original machine text and all retry/correction history remain intact. Possible identifying names are flagged locally for researcher review without silent redaction; this conservative heuristic can miss names and flag ordinary words. Review all transcripts.

`intake_transcripts.json` and `intake_transcripts.csv` are refreshed in the private session folder, tied to P### and the session UUID. The linked participant run export includes both files when its `desktop-session-id.txt` resolves to the matching local intake; otherwise it reports transcripts unavailable. Corrections change the export identity, preserving prior exports. Files contain no catalog name, are git-ignored, and must never be included in thesis outputs. Full field definitions are in [the intake data dictionary](../intake-data-dictionary.md). Withdrawal retains local audio/transcripts for the approved handling process and blocks new transcript operations and run exports; it does not delete prior exports or recall provider requests.

**Implemented service boundary:** generated follow-up methods and options are deleted. Intake, voice preview, transcription, cloning and cache synthesis use ElevenLabs only; ADB transfers go to the local headset. The retired cloud voice-prep scripts have been deleted. Study intake assignments lock guidance to Local MiniLM on the Mac through ADB. Optional Vercel guidance comparisons and Codex evaluation remain separate, explicitly invoked tools that can send guidance text/state externally; they do not ingest intake transcripts. ## Storage and recovery

**Implemented:** private desktop workspace `~/Documents/Trial Review Intake/<session UUID>/`, owner-only directory, contains `session.json`, immutable consent/eligibility receipts, question-specific capture authorization, original answer WAVs, optional multichannel `.source.wav` plus mono downmix, transcripts/quality/hash receipts, prompt/audition MP3s, clone input hash and answer IDs, `audio-cache/`, uniquely named audio manifests and a minimal assignment. The participant code is in state/manifests; the UUID prevents accidental directory reuse. Do not delete source recordings or provider voices as part of retries.

The clone uses a frozen snapshot of usable participant answer WAVs only. Prompts, tones, auditions and researcher comments are excluded. ASR is ElevenLabs `scribe_v2`; prompts and study synthesis use `eleven_turbo_v2_5`; cloning uses its existing instant voice-cloning endpoint. No intake answer is sent to another cloud provider. Credentials remain in the existing private `~/.config/follow-my-voice/api_keys.json`, outside source/app bundles.

The headset receives the minimal schema-2 assignment, full generated study-audio library and manifest. It does not receive raw answers, transcripts, consent-document reference, researcher initials or eligibility explanations. Consent ID, protocol version, reviewed-eligibility flag, cache manifest hash and development flag provide linkage. Participant/run `intake-assignment-v2.json` and summary `intake_assignment` preserve this provenance.

Closing/reopening the panel preserves worker results and saved state. Failed transcription can retry the saved answer without double-counting it. Uncertain clone outcomes do not auto-retry; the explicit retry warns of a possible duplicate provider voice. Worker failure does not authorize handoff. Withdrawal disables subsequent capture, synthesis and handoff but cannot undo a request already sent to a provider; retained data follows the approved handling process. Older protocol sessions remain read-only with their original version, prompts and transcripts; they are never relabelled as v3. Both v2 and v3 assignment contracts remain readable by the headset.

Development rehearsals must select **Development rehearsal** when reserving the session. Headset summaries use `session_mode=development_intake`, `study_data=false`, and retain the full two-block study path for testing. These are not participant observations. Ordinary quick-test modes remain separate.
