## Voice pipeline, verified in recovered source

`HintGenerator` chooses geometry-based semantic directions locally from the shared valid/fresh gaze and hidden target. `DirectionalHintPolicy` selects one of 44 guidance strings. `VoiceAssistantController.StarterPhrases` includes all directions and four success responses for both conditions. `VoiceSynthesizer` loads or generates ElevenLabs audio, prepares/levels clips, then uses the assigned voice's cache for hints. Startup generation can require network access; a prepared hint is local playback. Other allowed prompts may be prepared progressively/on demand.

Ordinary candidates stabilize for 150 ms and wait for the 1.25-second request interval plus an available speech channel. Area/return corrections require a prepared clip and recheck relevance before/during their 40 ms fade; obsolete ordinary guidance can be stopped directly. Invalid/stale gaze suspends new decisions. These settings are pilot values, not measured acoustic latency or validated eye events.

Voice wording is shared participant-directed imperative text. The existing self-similar-only `inner-thought-v2-soft-close` audio treatment remains in the recovered source; standard voice is dry. No new treatment was applied during recovery.
