# Accessibility rationale for spoken guidance

Status: cited precedent, not a new recruitment criterion or accessibility validation of Follow My Voice.

Coughlan, Biggs, Rivière and Shen (2020), *An Audio-Based 3D Spatial Guidance AR System for Blind Users*. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-58796-3_55); [author full-text PDF](https://ma-riviere.com/content/pubs/ICCHP20/CamIO.pdf).

Reviewed methods in sections 3–4 and outcomes in sections 5–6. The system gives cardinal-direction speech to guide a stylus to a physical object's hotspot. Four blind participants were studied; three already knew CamIO and one was a coauthor. This supports an accessibility precedent for speech-based spatial guidance. It does not establish greater benefit for low-vision users than for sighted users, nor feasibility of our eye-gaze-dependent system in this population. The abstract therefore uses the directly supported blind-user example instead of the comparative claim “especially helpful to those with partial blindness.”
