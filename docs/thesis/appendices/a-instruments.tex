\chapter{QUESTIONNAIRE WORDING AND ADMINISTRATION}\label{app:instruments}
\newpage
This appendix reproduces the proposed questionnaire wording, response ranges, and administration procedure. The condition-specific sets adapt NASA-TLX~\cite{hart1988nasatlx}, the Intrinsic Motivation Inventory~\cite{ryan1982control,mcauley1989imi}, and agent-perception items from Guo et al.~\cite{guo2024doppelganger}. The 0--10 NASA-TLX format and the task-specific wording are documented adaptations, and the Performance prompt asks about success and runs from Failure to Perfect. Synchronization of these forms with Qualtrics is on hold. Each condition-specific set refers to one block, but both sets are completed in the final survey, and no questionnaire is administered between blocks. Section~\ref{ch:methodology} and Appendix~\ref{app:data} give source attribution and scoring rules.

\section{Administration}
Participants will complete one practice trial in the first assigned voice, followed by two counterbalanced voice blocks of 40 measured trials each. The final survey will administer two separately labelled retrospective questionnaire sets, one for each voice block, with identical item wording and order. The break between blocks will include immediate comfort and tracking checks but no questionnaire. Survey administration and rest breaks both fall outside active-search timing.

Each condition-specific set in the final survey contains 65 numeric responses (130 across both sets) before technical checks and optional text. Setup will include a separate liking rating for each auditioned voice, including the self-similar voice.

\section{Retained Voice-Recording Passage}
The English passage below is reproduced verbatim from the run sheet and supplies the recording used to prepare the self-similar voice. Before participant use, the passage, recording duration, and audio acceptance procedure will be verified against the study build and versioned. The objects named in the passage are recording material only and do not identify the targets of measured trials. Participants will read in their usual speaking voice, without a request to whisper or imitate an inner monologue.

\begin{quote}
Locate the red sphere. Look toward the upper shelf. Move your gaze slowly to the right. Check the blue cube near the center. Compare each object's color and shape. Scan the lower row from left to right. Look beside the purple cylinder. Point the controller ray at the target and check its highlight. Press the trigger to select the matching object. Take a short pause. Get ready for the next trial.
\end{quote}
