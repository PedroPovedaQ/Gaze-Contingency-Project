\chapter{QUESTIONNAIRE WORDING AND ADMINISTRATION}\label{app:instruments}
\newpage
This appendix reproduces the proposed questionnaire wording, response ranges, and administration procedure. The condition-specific sets adapt NASA-TLX~\cite{hart1988nasatlx}, the Intrinsic Motivation Inventory~\cite{ryan1982control,mcauley1989imi}, agent-perception items from Guo et al.~\cite{guo2024doppelganger}, and draft Agent Rating Questionnaire items~\cite{wang2020terrible,venkatakrishnan2026clarifying}. The 0--10 NASA-TLX format and the task-specific wording are documented adaptations, and the Performance prompt asks about success and runs from Failure to Perfect. Synchronization of these forms with Qualtrics is on hold. Each condition-specific set refers to one block, but both sets are completed in the final survey, and no questionnaire is administered between blocks. Section~\ref{ch:methodology} and Appendix~\ref{app:data} give source attribution and scoring rules.

\section{Administration}
Participants will complete one practice trial in the first assigned voice, followed by two counterbalanced voice blocks of 40 measured trials each. Before the headset, an audio intake survey covers eligibility and background. The final survey presents each condition-specific item once, with side-by-side response columns for the Selected Voice and the Self-Similar Voice. The break between blocks will include immediate comfort and tracking checks but no questionnaire. Survey administration and rest breaks both fall outside active-search timing.

\tagpdfsetup{table/header-rows={1}}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}p{0.27\linewidth}X@{}}\toprule
Stage & Measures \\\midrule
After consent & Audio intake survey: eligibility, demographics and experience, spoken by a generic intake agent; answers also supply voice-cloning samples \\
Voice setup & Audition ratings and voice choice \\
Between voice blocks & Rest break, immediate comfort and tracking checks; no questionnaire \\
Final survey after both blocks & One side-by-side set rated for each voice (6 workload, 24 IMI, 21 agent-perception, 5 ARQ and 14 voice/assistance ratings per voice), technical checks, optional comments, voice comparison and debrief \\\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
Each voice column in the final survey contains 70 numeric responses (140 across both columns) before technical checks and optional text. Setup will include a separate liking rating for each auditioned voice, including the self-similar voice.

\section{Audio Intake Survey}
A generic intake agent, whose voice is not part of the neutral-voice catalog, reads each question aloud. A short tone signals when the participant should answer, and the researcher or system codes the spoken answer into the options shown. The participant's spoken answers also supply the speech samples used to clone the self-similar voice. An unanswered item remains missing.

\section{Fallback Voice-Recording Passage}
The participant's spoken answers during the audio intake normally supply the samples used to prepare the self-similar voice. The English passage below, reproduced verbatim from the run sheet, is read only when the intake does not yield enough usable speech.
