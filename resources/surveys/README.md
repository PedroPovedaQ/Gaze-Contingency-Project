The paper is methodological precedent, not the questionnaire itself. Section 4.3.3 (printed p. 238:10) uses interest/enjoyment, effort/importance, pressure/tension and value/usefulness. Section 5.2.5 reports no significant direct similarity-condition differences on those IMI outcomes. Its educational game and avatar differ from our MR search assistant; do not transfer its effects or interpretation of time played to our search times.
