# Guo et al. (2024): source survey, Table A1

**Protocol proposal — confirmed addition (2026-09-16):** include this survey in our study survey resources and measures planning. The full source instrument is retained below; final administration timing, item selection and voice-agent adaptations remain open. No original item has been silently removed or rewritten.

Source: Siqi Guo, Minsoo Choi, Dominic Kao, and Christos Mousas (2024). *Collaborating with my Doppelgänger: The Effects of Self-similar Appearance and Voice of a Virtual Character during a Jigsaw Puzzle Co-solving Task*. PACM Computer Graphics and Interactive Techniques 7(1), Article 4, 23 pages. https://doi.org/10.1145/3651288. Retrieved from https://web.ics.purdue.edu/~cmousas/papers/jour24-PACMCGIT-SelfSimiarAppearanceVoice.pdf on 2026-09-16. The table was checked against the PDF and the user-provided screenshot.

The paper reports 42 ratings across eight variables plus one open response, administered after each experimental condition (§3.4). It states that some source items were altered for its study. Citations below preserve the references attached to Table A1; their original instruments/scoring have not all been independently audited. This is a source transcription, not a claim that all eight adapted measures are independently validated for our task.

All Q1–Q42 anchors: **1: Very Strongly Disagree; 7: Very Strongly Agree**. Q43 is free text. Preserve source IDs in exports using prefix `GUO2024_` to distinguish them from IMI and other item numbers. A [CSV item bank](guo-2024-table-a1-items.csv) accompanies this transcription.

## Transfer to our voice-agent study

**Protocol proposal:** collect selected ratings after each voice block, with the same wording and order for both voices. Keep the whole source bank available until the participant form is finalized.

- “Virtual character” refers to an embodied collaborator in the source. Changing this to “voice assistant” is an adaptation: preserve original text, adapted text and rationale side by side in a later version.
- Q31 (physical attractiveness) and Q34–Q36 (appearance–voice correspondence) require visible appearance; mark them not applicable for a disembodied voice agent rather than assigning low scores. Final omission/adaptation remains open.
- Q15 assumes autonomous task pursuit; Q28–Q30 concern coworker/roommate/friend relationships. Pilot their interpretability for short search assistance. Perceived capabilities are participant beliefs, not proof of implemented capabilities.
- Co-presence concerns the perceived social other; it is not IPQ environmental presence. Attention to the assistant is not automatically beneficial when the task requires looking for objects.
- Q20 appealing, Q21 eerie and Q22 familiar are preserved as three distinct source items. Do not automatically average them into an eeriness score or substitute the legacy Ho & MacDorman semantic differential.
- Q32 general similarity is not an adequate replacement for the specific own-voice similarity manipulation check. Reconcile duplicate familiarity, helpfulness and naturalness questions across the battery.
- **Open scoring:** Table A1 supplies wording/anchors, not a complete scoring key. Retain raw responses and missing/not-applicable flags. Verify original scale aggregation and reverse coding before computing scale scores (especially negatively phrased Q7, Q8, Q11 and Q12). No overall 42-item total. Q19 is a single study-created rating; Q43 is qualitative.
- All 42 ratings plus the proposed 24-item IMI and six TLX dimensions would total **72 ratings per block**, before other checks. Time this battery in rehearsal and freeze a justified selection before participant collection. No burden estimate in minutes is asserted.
